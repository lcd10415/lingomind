Built for how memory actually works 按记忆真正运作的方式设计

Not another flashcard pile. A short daily loop that starts from sentences you chose yourself. 不是又一堆闪卡。每天短循环，从你自己选中的句子开始。

01 · Capture

Save sentences from real reading 从真实阅读中采集句子

Paste from clipboard, type an idiom, or snap a photo with on-device OCR. Context stays with the expression — the way you first met it. 粘贴剪贴板、手输习语，或用设备端 OCR 拍照。语境与表达一起保存——就是你初次遇见它的样子。

02 · Sense & Recall

Understand, then actively recall 先理解，再主动回忆

Sense the meaning in context, then prove you remember it. Spaced stages move expressions from Active Recall to Reinforced to Mastered. 在语境中理解含义，再证明你记得住。间隔复习把表达从主动回忆推进到巩固与掌握。

03 · Produce & Verify

Write your own sentence 写出属于你的句子

Production cements usage. Draft an original sentence, then verify collocation and tone when you are ready — optional AI feedback, always on your terms. 产出才能把用法钉牢。起草原句，再按需验证搭配与语气——可选 AI 反馈，节奏由你掌控。

Today, library, and progress at a glance 今日、词库与进度一目了然

A calm daily desk: what is due, what you captured, and how memory is shifting. 安静的每日书桌：待复习、已采集，以及记忆状态如何变化。

Local-first by design 本地优先，天生如此

Capture, Sense, Recall, Produce, and Verify run on your device. Your reading stays yours. 采集、理解、回忆、产出、验证均在设备本地运行。你的阅读内容只属于你。

  • Clipboard is read only when you tap paste 仅在你点击粘贴时读取剪贴板
  • Photos and OCR stay on-device 照片与 OCR 均在本机处理
  • iCloud sync optional — off by default iCloud 同步可选，默认关闭

Start with one sentence 从一句话开始

A few minutes a day. Vocabulary from the language you actually read and write. 每天几分钟。词汇来自你真正在读、在写的语言。
